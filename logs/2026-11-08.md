# 📝 Daily Log — 8 November 2026

## 💡 Concept of the Day

### Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) — put your site's files closer to your users

When someone visits your site, every image, stylesheet, and script travels from your origin server to their browser. If that server sits in Mumbai and the visitor is in Berlin, the data crosses the world — and every kilometre adds latency.

A Content Delivery Network (CDN) fixes this by caching copies of your content on **edge servers** spread across the globe. A visitor is served from the nearest edge server instead of the origin, so the trip is short and the page loads fast.

How it works:

- **First request (cache miss):** the edge server fetches the file from your origin, serves it, and keeps a copy.
- **Later requests (cache hit):** the edge server serves its copy instantly — your origin never hears about it.
- **Static assets benefit most:** images, CSS, JavaScript, and videos rarely change, so they are ideal for caching.

Why it matters:

- **Faster load times.** Less distance means lower latency, which keeps visitors from bouncing.
- **Lower bandwidth bills.** Cached requests never reach your origin, so you pay for far less egress.
- **Resilience.** Traffic spikes and hardware failures are absorbed across many servers instead of hammering one.
- **Security.** Because a CDN sits in front of your origin, it can absorb DDoS traffic and manage TLS certificates for you.

One thing to watch: **cache invalidation.** A cached file can go stale after you deploy a fix. Purge the cache (or version your asset filenames, like `app.v2.js`) so users get the new file.

## 🛠️ Tool of the Day

### Cloudflare CDN — a global CDN with a free tier

Cloudflare runs one of the largest CDNs in the world, caching content in data centres in 335+ cities. Unlike old-school CDNs, it works as a reverse proxy: you point your domain's DNS at Cloudflare and it starts caching — no URL changes needed.

Why it's worth trying:

- **Free plan** includes the CDN plus DDoS protection — enough for a personal project or portfolio.
- **Zero-config caching** for static assets, with cache rules and instant purging when you need control.
- **Bonus features** on the same setup: free TLS certificates, analytics, and a firewall.

How to try it:

1. Create a free account at cloudflare.com and add your site.
2. Change your domain's nameservers to the ones Cloudflare gives you.
3. Visit your site and check the `cf-cache-status` response header — `HIT` means the edge served it.
